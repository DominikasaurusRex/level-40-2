# Level 40-2 — Story and Progression

## Premise

The player is an adventurer called to a small town recently devastated by an enormous creature. The town still has its walls and gate, but its buildings are broken. Materials and gold gathered during runs allow the player to repair and rebuild it.

Some monsters from the original Level 40 will return, alongside new monsters suited to the fire, ice and thunder system. From stage 11 onward, the roster can increasingly feature new enemies. The journey will not lead to Hell or the Abyss.

Elemental emphasis describes the area's creatures and attacks, not a fixed weakness chart. All three elements should have useful matchups across runs and remain useful near the end; every area does not need equal representation.

The split within paired five-stage areas is flexible: aim for roughly 4–5 elemental attackers across stages 11–20, 5–6 across 21–30 and 6–7 across 31–40. These targets describe increasing elemental presence, not a requirement to remove physical attacks.

Balance elemental opportunities across the run rather than making every area evenly mixed. Across the final ten stages, each element should have at least two worthwhile offensive targets. A useful matchup can mean a weakness, or normal damage where another element is resisted. Consider encounter difficulty and duration as well as raw counts.

Early weaknesses need not come with resistances. Introduce resistance, immunity and absorption more selectively as player options expand. Avoid long late-game stretches that make one element consistently ineffective, especially because equipped weapons cannot be deactivated. Exact resistances and superboss affinities will be decided with the individual rosters.

## Stage 25 — Apparent Victory

The destroyer is envisioned as an enormous storm-bearing guardian, with Rayquaza as a loose reference. It was trying to protect the world from the greater threat, rather than fleeing it. The storms accompanying its journey devastated the town as collateral damage.

After its defeat, a cutscene shows the town celebrating the adventurer. Questions remain about why the creature climbed so high, and it becomes clear that something greater is coming. This is the initial **“Fin?”** ending, moved to stage 25.

A possible final image is a starry sky with a giant eye opening on the moon. The exact scene and how the guardian was protecting the world remain open.

## Space and the True Ending

The player goes to space after the summit temple, crossing a broken floating temple and the moon’s surface before entering the moon to confront the threat within.

The final encounters progress from the **Moon’s Core** at stage 40 to the **Moon’s Shadow** at 41 and **the Moon itself** at 42. Defeating the final superboss leads to **“Fin!”**.

Exact monster identities, area rosters, the transition into space and the connections between the final three bosses remain to be developed. These are the current working story and area choices.
